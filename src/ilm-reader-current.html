Reading Kitāb al-ʿIlm

The introduction and commentary on all 83 numbered entries, 198–280, are included, along with the supplementary cross-reference after 248. Original Arabic opens first; use Vocalised Arabic to switch the same passage to the supplied reading draft. Select the Arabic passage to reveal English directly below. Study mode opens all commentary translations.

Hadith Arabic and English are separate, uncoloured blocks. Hadiths 198–218 use the supplied Review 06 matn and James Robson English. Later matn follows the saved Mirqāt transcription, retaining its short references and repetitions. No new vocalisation or translation has been composed for this edition.

Review status and source notes

The complete commentary comes from the saved D3 Complete / 01 pack, dated 24 September 2026. Its source Arabic, vocalised reading, English, stable IDs, source references and all 450 editorial notes are retained. D4 review and E scholarly verification remain pending. Each passage retains its supplied status and notes.

Hadith English for 219–280 remains pending; the commentary translations are available in full.

Source: Mirqāt al-Mafātīḥ, volume 1, Jamāl ʿItānī edition, Dār al-Kutub al-ʿIlmiyyah, 1422 AH / 2001 CE. Bushra remains a comparison witness for the hadith matn only. Review notes preserve unresolved readings and differences between witnesses.
